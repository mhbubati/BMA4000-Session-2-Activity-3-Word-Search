💡 Why This Matters

This is not just a word search — it is a vocabulary builder for your future career. These 16 terms from Sessions 1 & 2 are the foundation language of business analysis. Mastering them means you can:

  • Analyse business environments using PESTLE, MACRO, MESO, and MICRO frameworks
  • Map stakeholders using Mendelow's Matrix (Power × Interest)
  • Classify uncertainty as Simple/Static, Dynamic, or Complex
  • Discuss business transformation, value creation, and stakeholder management with confidence

📖 How to Play — Two Easy Methods

👆
Method 1: Tap-Tap 📱 Best for Mobile
Step 1: Tap the first letter of the word (it turns gold)
Step 2: Tap the last letter of the word
The game draws the line between them automatically!
Letters shared between words can be tapped again.
🖱️
Method 2: Click & Drag 💻 Best for Desktop
Step 1: Click and hold the first letter
Step 2: Drag to the last letter and release
📏 Rules: Words go across → or down ↓ only (no diagonals). Words share letters at intersections. 💡 Hint adds 10 seconds. Find all 16 words to win!

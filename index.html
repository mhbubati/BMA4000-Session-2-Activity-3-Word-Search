🔍 Business Environment Word Search

Find 16 key business terms hidden in the grid. Each word is colour-coded by environment, and every word you find reveals a plain-English definition with a real UK example.

💡 Why This Matters

This is not just a word search — it is a vocabulary builder for your Integrated Professional Analysis. These 16 terms from Sessions 5–7 (and previewed for Session 9–10) are the working language of business analysis. Mastering them means you can:

  • Analyse the external environment using the PESTLE framework — Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental
  • Explain how inflation, interest rates, and recession shape business decisions
  • Discuss legal compliance, liability, and regulation with confidence
  • Evaluate digital transformation, innovation, and automation in any sector

"The limits of my language mean the limits of my world." — Ludwig Wittgenstein

💡 Session 7 reminder: CHASM and DIFFUSION come from Moore's and Rogers' theories — the two adoption models we covered in Session 7. If you missed that session, review it — they're essential for your report.
